Mostly me, plus whoever turns up.

I am Iván Alonso, **neverbot**. I started playing [muds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-user_dungeon) with _Reinos de Leyenda_ around 1998, became a `creator` (coder), and ended up an `alchemist` (lesser administrator) before the mud closed its doors. With a few other coders I was rewriting its mudlib when that happened. The rewrite never opened to the public, but several of us kept our backups and, years later, turned them into versions of our own: the people behind _Reinos de Leyenda: Los Años Oscuros_ did it, and so did I with [_Reinos de Leyenda: Aeternum_](/games/#Hexagon), which runs on [Hexagon](https://github.com/maldorne/hexagon) today.

That habit is this whole place: a game of my own since January 2003, a mudlib underneath it, and old games whose only surviving backup would otherwise sit in a drawer, [booted again and left open](/games/).

What I do away from muds is at [neverbot.com](https://neverbot.com).
